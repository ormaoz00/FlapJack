❓ FAQ

Got Questions?

Everything you need to know about FlapJack oat bars.

Unlike mass-produced bars, each FlapJack is handmade in a small facility in Hungary using a traditional recipe. We use 42-47% premium English oat flakes — not cheap fillers. The result is a bar with real texture, rich flavor, and quality you can genuinely feel. Made by S.P.I.N. Ltd., a trusted European food producer since 2004.
Yes! FlapJack bars are suitable for vegetarians. They're made with recognizable, real ingredients including oat flakes, vegetable oils, demerara sugar, and natural flavor additions like real fruit, nuts, and cocoa. Select flavors are also palm oil-free. Always check the specific flavor's label for allergen information.
Each 100g FlapJack bar contains approximately 450-459 kcal of balanced energy from complex and simple carbohydrates. The 60g bars contain proportionally less. The Low GI formula means sustained fuel without sugar spikes or crashes — perfect as a meal replacement or energy boost.
Allergens vary by flavor. Some flavors contain milk, soy, and tree nuts. The Toffee flavor also contains wheat flour (gluten). All flavors may contain traces of pistachios and other nuts. Please always check the specific product label for your chosen variety's detailed allergen information.
FlapJack bars are handmade in Hungary under strict European food safety regulations. S.P.I.N. Ltd. has been producing and distributing premium food products across Central and Eastern Europe since 2004. The oats are sourced from England for the highest quality.
FlapJack bars are available in two sizes: 100g (our standard bar, perfect as a meal replacement or substantial snack) and 60g (a lighter option for the Sports & Special Edition flavors). Both sizes are individually wrapped for freshness and portability.
